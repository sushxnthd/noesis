Question: Can an exact task-coupled diagnostic find the globally shortest mixed-word failure in ordinary gradient-trained recurrent networks?
Frontier: El-Naggar et al., ICGI 2023; Dave et al., NeSy 2025; Buitrago and Gu, ICML 2025.
Success: independently verify the exact shortest error > 0.1 for all three selected trained checkpoints.
Competitor: elementary one-dimensional counter error algebra, not general learned algorithmicity.
Falsifier: independently verify off-ray guards, all shorter-length upper bounds, and explicit witness.
Publishable threshold: novel priority-audited prospective result across unseen architectures and tasks.
Learning threshold: ordinary trained checkpoints and a changed worst-case evaluation practice.
Kill: checker mismatch, reachable off-ray activation, or three failed attempts to generalize beyond exact rays.
