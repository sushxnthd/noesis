# NOESIS — Active Kernel 001 after Run294
Status: reformulated, not promoted to a field-level breakthrough. Exactly one active kernel. Two backups remain inactive.

1. **Question:** Can a task-observable, representation-robust inductive simulation score predict when gradient-trained neural networks acquire unrestricted correctness on a new algorithmic task, rather than merely predict proximity to a box verifier?
2. **Frontier:** RNN invariant inference (Jacoby et al., ATVA 2020), polyhedral RNN verification (Ryou et al., CAV 2021), formal-language RNN counting (El-Naggar et al., ICGI 2023), neural abstraction and realization equivalence. NOESIS Runs289,292,294 falsify successive representation-independent interpretations.
3. **Exact primary success criterion:** Prospectively frozen diagnostic beats early training loss by ROC-AUC >=0.10 on at least 20 independently certified-correct and 30 exactly witnessed-incorrect endpoints of an unseen algorithmic task, with unresolved models abstained.
4. **Competing explanation:** Diagnostic predicts the particular verifier's acceptance rather than true algorithmicity; it exploits task-specific label or architecture priors.
5. **Cheapest falsification:** Freeze task-observable pruning and scoring before endpoint labels; evaluate strongest silent-clock, duplication, gauge and finite-horizon spoof controls, then independent correctness outcomes.
6. **Publishable threshold:** Independent checker, primary-priority audit, precommitted holdout, spoof-resistant behavior, unseen-condition prediction, smallest claim, and trained-model relevance.
7. **Learning relevance threshold:** At least two gradient-training transitions from exact incorrectness witnesses to independently certified all-length correctness in the unseen task; no post-hoc selection.
8. **Kill:** Three substantive failed improvements, a false certificate, or no advantage over loss on adequately resolved endpoints. Reformulate rather than rebrand a finite-range metric.

Inactive backups: (a) causal intervention on certified transition; (b) reachable-state relational abstraction beyond box certificates.
