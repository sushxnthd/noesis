# Smallest supported claim
For one previously gradient-trained 4-state ReLU network (Run147 seed 214), exact outward interval enumeration of all ternary sequences and independent rational witness checking establish that the globally shortest sequence with counting error strictly greater than 0.1 has length 13. Its previously certified repeated-plus first-failure length is 125. Therefore a correct inductive certificate on a genuinely multidimensional learned *single-token* face is not a sufficient task-level certificate. This is a retrospective negative evaluation result, not a novel model-agnostic law or proof of complete algorithm acquisition.
