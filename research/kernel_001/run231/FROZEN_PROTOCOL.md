## Precise question
Can an automatically discovered **signed-coordinate relational quotient** recover already-established all-length neural algorithm certificates under exact, function-preserving negated-coordinate augmentations that evade the Run 230 equality-only quotient?

## Exact protocol
For each of 12 independently gradient-trained, all-length-certified RNN checkpoints, create TWO 1-coordinate augmentations with s_t=-h_{t,0}:
(A) signed duplicate with new tanh preactivation equal to negative of original coordinate 0; (B) off-manifold-sheared signed duplicate with terms proportional to h_0+s (which vanish on reachable states), with matching negative shear in the duplicate row. Use K=2^14 and readout z'=z+K(s+h_0). Use exact Fraction representations of checkpoint float64 weights.
The discovery algorithm may inspect only augmented parameters and initial state, not original weights/certificates or generator labels. Search all signed coordinate pairs, check exact preactivation compatibility, quotient by substitution, and independently audit the relation. Only after quotient discovery, compare with source and replay the pre-existing rigorous all-length certificate. As negative controls, corrupt duplicate row, token bias, and initial coordinate by +1/8; require abstention. Also run the previous equality-only quotient and record whether it abstains.

## Scalar success criterion
24/24 signed quotient recoveries AND 3/3 mutated controls abstain. All outputs, including failures, must be recorded.
