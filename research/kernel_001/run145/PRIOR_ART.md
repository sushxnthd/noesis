# Primary literature and priority limits
- El-Naggar et al. (2023), exact counting conditions for neural counters: https://proceedings.mlr.press/v217/el-naggar23a.html
- Barabanov & Prokhorov (2002), Stability analysis of discrete-time recurrent neural networks, IEEE TNN, DOI 10.1109/72.991416. Uses absolute-stability and LMI machinery beyond naive raw eigenvalues.
- Vahedi Noori, Hu, Dullerud & Seiler (2024), Stability and Performance Analysis of Discrete-Time ReLU Recurrent Neural Networks: https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.05236 . Uses Lyapunov/dissipativity and ReLU quadratic constraints.
The nonlinear stability distinction is established in prior art. Our contribution is an exact arithmetic witness **inside an ordinarily trained counting checkpoint**, not a new stability law. Priority audit incomplete for a publishable claim.
