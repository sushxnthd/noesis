# NOESIS Kernel 001 — post-Run300 decision (one active kernel)

**Decision:** KILL the Run291 gauge-fixed box-LP score as a cross-task, model-agnostic algorithmicity predictor. Run300 prospectively showed AUC 0.33765 vs 0.92336 for early loss on exactly resolved latch networks (17 exact correct, 175 exact wrong, 192 unresolved). Retain **Kernel 001 only in reformulated form**, focused on learned task-observable algorithm acquisition.

1. **Precise question.** Can a reachable-state, task-observable, functionally representation-robust diagnostic predict *unrestricted correctness of gradient-trained models* beyond the task-specific training-loss baseline, without relying on a selected easy-to-certify subset?
2. **Primary-literature frontier.** Jacoby–Barrett–Katz (ATVA 2020) already develop RNN invariant inference; Zhang et al. (ECAI 2020) apply reachability/invariant methods; Khmelnitsky et al. (ATVA 2021) property-directed verification. NOESIS cannot claim inductive invariants themselves as new.
3. **One scalar success criterion.** In a *prospectively frozen*, genuinely new architecture/task condition, diagnostic ROC-AUC minus epoch20 negative-BCE ROC-AUC >= +0.10 on independently resolved endpoints; no unresolved relabeling. Require >=10 exact positives and >=50 exact negatives to evaluate.
4. **Strongest competing explanation.** Any apparent predictive gain is just training loss, parameterization, task-specific monotone growth, or exact-verifier selection bias.
5. **Cheapest decisive falsification.** Before training another large cohort, test whether the proposed score is invariant to the exact function-preserving augmentations from Runs292–296 and whether its ranking changes under plausible missing-label assignments for the 192 Run300 unresolved models. A failed test kills the candidate without expensive training.
6. **Publishable threshold.** Primary literature priority audit; exact proof/independent checker; prospective result; strong spoof baselines; representation robustness; new task/architecture transfer; smallest-claim manuscript; public CI evidence.
7. **Learning relevance threshold.** Predictions must concern new gradient-trained checkpoints, with >=10 unrestricted-correctness certificates and >=50 exact incorrectness witnesses; additionally track sampled-perfect but wrong networks and at least one independently certified training transition before causal acquisition claims.
8. **Explicit kill condition.** Kill a candidate after one exact function-preserving spoof or after three substantive failures to improve prospectively measured predictive value. If this reformulated K001 fails three attempts, terminate K001 rather than drifting into abstract automata theory.

**Backups (inactive, max two):** (A) learned acquisition-transition detection from checkpoint pairs with exact positive/negative endpoints; (B) representation-independent reachable-state quotient. Wildcards remain isolated and cannot change frozen evaluations.
