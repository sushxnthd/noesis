# Run204 THEORY

For a deterministic task whose output is its complete state, finite correctness through length H+1 implies output-label transition congruence for every nonempty prefix through length H. If f(u)=f(v)=q and both u and v are correct, then f(ua)=delta(q,a)=f(va) whenever the appended words are also correct. Therefore an observed quotient violation implies an observed finite prediction error.

For CountMod3, if the first error occurs at length L>=4, choose u as the failing word's prefix and a as its last bit. Choose a correct representative v in {0,1,11} with the same residue as u. Since |va|<=3<L, f(ua) differs from f(va). Thus earliest quotient violation depth D=L-1. The reverse bound follows from finite correctness through L-1. This is a general logical implication, not a new neural learning law.

Finite k-suffix signatures similarly contain no all-length proof without an independently verified closure invariant. Kill finite output-only quotients as primary acquisition certificates, not as finite error detectors.
