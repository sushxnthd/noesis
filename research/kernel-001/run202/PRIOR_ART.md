# Primary-literature priority boundary for Run202 / next kernel

- Weiss, Goldberg, Yahav, *Extracting Automata from Recurrent Neural Networks Using Queries and Counterexamples*, ICML 2018: https://proceedings.mlr.press/v80/weiss18a.html . Existing abstraction/automata extraction from trained RNNs.
- Jacoby, Barrett, Katz, *Verifying Recurrent Neural Networks Using Invariant Inference*, ATVA 2020: https://theory.stanford.edu/~barrett/pubs/JBK20-abstract.html . Invariant inference for RNN verification.
- Ryou et al., *Scalable Polyhedral Verification of Recurrent Neural Networks*, CAV 2021: https://www.sri.inf.ethz.ch/publications/ryou2021scalable . Polyhedral verification and abstraction refinement.
- Van Rossem and Saxe, *Algorithm Development in Neural Networks: Insights from the Streaming Parity Task*, ICML 2025: https://proceedings.mlr.press/v267/van-rossem25a.html . Mechanistic study of infinite-generalization transitions.

These papers preclude claiming that inductive RNN verification, automata extraction, or parity acquisition is novel by itself. NOESIS must demonstrate new prospective, spoof-resistant certification of actual learning transitions.
