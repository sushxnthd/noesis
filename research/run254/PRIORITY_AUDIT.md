# Preliminary priority audit, 2026-10-19
Van Rossem and Saxe, ICML 2025 (https://proceedings.mlr.press/v267/van-rossem25a.html), already report learned infinite parity generalization. Butoi et al., 2024 (https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.07107) study neural formal-language recognition. Dave et al., NeSy 2025 (https://proceedings.mlr.press/v284/dave25a.html) study finite-precision RNN learnability. Czerner et al., CONCUR 2024 (https://drops.dagstuhl.de/entities/document/10.4230/LIPIcs.CONCUR.2024.19) establish inductive abstractions. Lin and Hong, 2026 (https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.12490), study RNN activation refinement. Do not claim novelty of polyhedral verification, algorithm acquisition, or infinite generalization. Prospective multi-model transfer and mechanism are still missing.
