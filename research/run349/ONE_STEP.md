# Run349 post-hoc follow-up: one-step repairability gain
Original seed 32135: epoch 82 has an exact odd/even convex-hull collision on four frozen-generator words of maximum length 23. Thus no affine readout can correctly classify those four states. At epoch 83, an independently recomputed exact rational affine separator classifies **all 2,644** frozen-generator words of length <=23 with strictly positive signed margin (minimum approximately 1.0). Both checkpoints have 100% training accuracy; BCE falls from 0.19951183031058095 to 0.18548062428707512. This establishes a one-gradient-step change in finite-generator readout repairability despite identical training accuracy. It does not prove that unrestricted algorithmic correctness emerged at epoch 83; the same trajectory is certified unrestricted-correct at epoch 200. The one-step analysis was performed **after** the Run349 frozen scan and is retrospective, not a prospectively frozen prediction. The exact separator is synthesized numerically and then verified with rational arithmetic on all 2,644 states.
