# NOESIS K001 Run288 — prospectively frozen protocol
Frozen 2026-10-20 before any Run288 training or endpoint labels were computed. This protocol is a direct K001-R continuation; no second active kernel.

1. **Precise question.** Does an early relaxed two-box inductive-invariant feasibility distance at epoch 20 predict **later discovery of an exact unrestricted correctness certificate** at epoch 80 in fresh, neutrally initialized, gradient-trained width-two ReLU recurrent networks, better than sampled binary cross-entropy? Certificate discovery is NOT equated with behavioral correctness when absent.
2. **Primary-literature frontier.** Neural formal-language generalization (Butoi et al., ICLR 2025), metric automata/RNN robustness (Dankowiakowski & Ronca, NeurIPS 2025), neural-symbolic learnability (Dave et al., NeSy 2025). Prior art not cleared. This is a discriminative diagnostic test, not a claim of first neural algorithm acquisition.
3. **Single scalar success criterion.** On 256 fresh seeds 20928..21183 inclusive, among all networks, ROC-AUC(-LP-distance, exact-certificate-at-epoch80) minus ROC-AUC(-BCE-at-epoch20, same endpoint) >= 0.10, with >=15 exact certificates, >=30 noncertificates, diagnostic ROC-AUC >=0.70, and zero independently verified certificate soundness failures. All conditions required. The endpoint is *certifiability* only; noncertificates are unknown, not incorrect.
4. **Strongest competing explanation.** The LP distance predicts the LP verifier's own ability to find boxes (certificate-family circularity), not true unrestricted correctness or an acquisition transition. Another competing explanation is sampled training accuracy or parameter norm.
5. **Cheapest decisive falsification.** Train fresh cohort with frozen architecture/optimizer, evaluate early distance without reading later checkpoints, then compute epoch80 LP certificates and independent rational checker. Compare frozen BCE and sampled-accuracy baselines. All unknown correctness outcomes remain unknown.
6. **Publishable threshold.** Primary criterion plus independent public reproduction, stronger spoof baselines, a prospectively frozen cross-architecture or cross-task test, primary-priority audit, and mechanism-derived prediction. This single run alone cannot establish a field-level breakthrough.
7. **Learning relevance threshold.** All 256 networks must be genuinely trained with ordinary BCE/Adam. To claim *algorithm acquisition prediction*, additionally require >=5 early sampled-perfect exact-incorrect -> late exact-correct transitions and a discriminative test among both exact-correct and exact-incorrect late networks. Otherwise report only certificate-onset prediction.
8. **Kill condition.** If primary fails or improvement vanishes on true correctness labels, retire LP distance as an acquisition predictor; do not tune the score on held-out outcomes. After one more frozen failure kill/reformulate K001-R.

**Fixed cohort and model:** width-two, symbol-conditioned ReLU recurrence with 15 float64 parameters; Gaussian init sigma=0.5, seed+51000; 160 random six-symbol strings plus four fixed two-symbol prefixes; per-token BCE, Adam lr=.025, epochs 0..120, snapshots [0,20,40,80,120]; torch deterministic, one thread. Reproduce original Run286 training implementation with only seed range changed. No user laptop, no paid API.

**Fixed diagnostic:** For each of four two-box masks (state0 coordinates free, state1 mask bits), minimize nonnegative common slack t in the same linear inequalities as Run286's sufficient certificate LP; add -t to every transition lower/upper and output-separation inequality, enforce logit separation delta=0.05, enforce state0 initial box at zero, 0<=L<=U<=1000, t>=0. Early score is negative minimum t over four masks. Use scipy.optimize.linprog(method='highs'); an infeasible mask contributes +infinity. No fitted thresholds, coefficients, or post-hoc mask selection. Pilot used to choose this score: old Run286 cohort only, exploratory AUC distance 0.80625 versus BCE 0.36536; those data are NOT confirmatory.

**Fixed verification:** Use Run286 certificate_lp/find_cert at epoch80; independently check every returned box with exact Fraction/SymPy. Check for exact witnesses via RESET+TOGGLE^n up to 256 and 256 seeded mixed words each at lengths 64 and 128. Witnesses are one-sided proof of incorrectness; no witness is not proof of correctness. No conflicting certificate/witness accepted. Report per-seed all checkpoints, outcomes, raw scores, abstentions, AUC, controls, scripts, environment and SHA256. Preserve all negative outcomes.

**Frozen decision:** Positive only if every numerical condition in item 3 passes. No changes to diagnostic after seeing new endpoint labels.
