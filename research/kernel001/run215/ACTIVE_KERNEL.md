# NOESIS active kernel 001 — certificate-aware temporal acquisition

**One active kernel only.** Naive finite-horizon transition closure is falsified as a sufficient certificate by the exact capped-neural-recurrence spoof from PR #1. The replacement is an inductive full-alphabet behavioral certificate paired with a pre-acquisition diagnostic on actual gradient-trained systems.

1. **Precise question:** Can a finite-budget, architecture-agnostic diagnostic measured at training epoch t predict a later *independently verified incorrect-to-all-length-correct* transition, not merely endpoint correctness?
2. **Primary-literature frontier:** Jacoby, Barrett & Katz, *Verifying Recurrent Neural Networks Using Invariant Inference* (ATVA 2020); Michalenko et al., *Representing Formal Languages* (2019); Mohammadinejad et al., *DiffRNN* (2020). RNN invariant inference and automata extraction are not new. Run214's matched-budget generic margin beat retention; Run215's temporal audit is weak and in-cohort.
3. **One success criterion for next frozen test:** On a genuinely unseen neural architecture with >=4 verified transitions and >=4 persistent failures, generic-prefix margin measured before acquisition exceeds the strongest equal-budget baseline by **AUROC >=0.10**.
4. **Strongest competing explanation:** Minimum margin merely measures finite-training convergence or architecture-specific calibration, rather than acquisition.
5. **Cheapest decisive falsification:** Train a small, frozen-seed unseen recurrent architecture; certify all-length endpoint behavior, verify earlier errors with two independent checkers, compare generic-prefix against training loss, finite heldout accuracy, and matched-budget generic final margin.
6. **Publishable threshold:** Independent reproducibility, strong adversarial spoof baselines, prospective held-out prediction, ablation of architecture/task, explicit smallest claim and novelty audit.
7. **Learning relevance threshold:** At least four actual incorrect-to-correct transitions with independently verified behavior; abstentions excluded from binary labels.
8. **Kill condition:** Three substantive prospective failures to beat the strongest baseline, or inability to obtain verified transitions in a feasible cohort. Retire generic-prefix predictor, not the valid inductive certificates.

**Backups:** None active. **Theory ledger:** invariant certificates and finite-horizon spoof limits. **Learning ledger:** verified checkpoint transitions and predictors. No causal acquisition law established.
