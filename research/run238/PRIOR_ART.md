# Primary-literature boundary
Wang & Niepert (ICML 2019): https://proceedings.mlr.press/v97/wang19j.html — state-regularized recurrent networks and automata extraction.
Oliva & Lago-Fernández (2020): https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.10828 — saturation and stable recurrent states.
Van Rossem & Saxe (ICML 2025): https://proceedings.mlr.press/v267/van-rossem25a.html — algorithm-development transitions and infinite generalization.
These precedents rule out novelty claims based only on saturation, automata formation, or all-length correctness.
