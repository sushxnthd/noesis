Assume u,v are full-DFA-state-neutral; oracle suffix-1 readout is strictly correct on all boundary boxes; and for every source ab, word c and proper prefix length t∈{1,...,19}, the entire interval-enclosed image of B_ab under c[:t] has strictly correct oracle suffix-1 readout. Then every arbitrary infinite concatenation of u/v after either certified entry has correct oracle suffix-1 readout at every boundary and every proper within-word cut. Proof: induction along the original graph establishes boundary regions, and uniform prefix-image inequalities establish interior cuts.
