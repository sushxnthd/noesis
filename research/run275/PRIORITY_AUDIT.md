# Primary-literature priority audit (Run275, 2026-10-20)
- Van Rossem & Saxe, *Algorithm Development in Neural Networks: Insights from the Streaming Parity Task*, ICML 2025, https://proceedings.mlr.press/v267/van-rossem25a.html. Already reports RNN transitions from finite training to perfect infinite generalization, with a representation-merger mechanism. **Collision:** NOESIS cannot claim that discovering natural acquisition transitions is novel.
- Ryou et al., *Scalable Polyhedral Verification of Recurrent Neural Networks*, CAV 2021, https://www.sri.inf.ethz.ch/publications/ryou2021scalable. **Collision:** Certifying recurrent systems via inductive abstract regions is not new.
- Dan, Bastani, Roth, *Understanding Robust Generalization in Learning Regular Languages* (2022), https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.09717. Auxiliary DFA-state supervision improves systematic generalization; this is relevant to intervention baselines.
**Smallest supported NOESIS claim:** In a new 128-seed cohort, four BCE-only gradient trajectories are exactly proven to move from sampled-perfect but incorrect to all-alphabet inductively certified correct on a latch task. This is a reproducible demonstration and an evaluation warning, not a new general law.
**Unresolved novelty requirement:** Prospectively frozen diagnostic predicts transitions on unseen architectures/tasks better than trivial finite-accuracy and coefficient baselines; independent checker and strong spoof controls.
