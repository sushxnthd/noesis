# Run250 priority collision
- Van Rossem & Saxe, ICML 2025, Algorithm Development in Neural Networks: Insights from the Streaming Parity Task (https://proceedings.mlr.press/v267/van-rossem25a.html): learning transitions and finite automata are established.
- Buitrago & Gu, ICML 2025, Understanding and Improving Length Generalization in Recurrent Models (https://proceedings.mlr.press/v267/buitrago25a.html): unexplored reachable states are already a length-generalization explanation.
- Choi et al., 2025, Reachability Analysis of Recurrent Neural Networks (https://doi.org/10.1016/j.nahs.2025.101581): recurrent reachability verification has established methods.
The Run250 result is a prospective adversarial evaluation control, not a new universal mechanism or certificate.
