6. **Publishable threshold:** Independent reproduction, primary-literature priority audit, adversarial spoofs, unseen architecture/task transfer, mechanistic prediction, smallest-claim report.
7. **Learning-system relevance:** Predict epoch-later behavior of actual gradient-trained networks; proof acceptance alone is insufficient, and abstentions remain unresolved.
