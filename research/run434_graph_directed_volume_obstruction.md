## Mathematical statement and soundness boundary

For compact convex graph boxes B_i in R^d and C1 edge maps F_e:B_source -> B_target, let K_i be the unique graph-directed compact attractors when all edge maps are uniformly contractive. For each edge choose c_e >= sup_{B_source}|det D F_e|. Define C_(target,source)=sum of c_e over edges from source to target. The area formula yields v_i=Leb_d(K_i) satisfying v<=Cv. If max column sum(C)<1, summing components forces v_i=0 for all nodes. This remains true when the original invariant boxes themselves have positive ambient volume. It does not imply an empty attractor or failure to observe/control within the attractor. All bounds are over the entire unchanged original boxes, not point-Jacobian estimates.

The full word Jacobian is propagated by MPFR directed 1024-bit interval dual arithmetic using exact-dyadic serialized float64 weights. The determinant upper uses |det J|<=product_i(sum_j sup|J_ij|); outward MPFR endpoints are converted to outward binary64, then sums/products/comparisons use exact Fraction arithmetic. Uniform edge contraction uses the same rigorous row sums. Conditional exact-real validity depends on the old R418 MPFR graph inclusion, and **does not prove IEEE/PyTorch floating-point execution**.
